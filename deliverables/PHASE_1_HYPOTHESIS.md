# Phase 1 — Investigate: initial decision hypothesis

**Role:** Quantitative investment researcher. **Decision:** whether to invest $3M at $25M pre-money in the fictional company's NY–London entanglement-based coordination service.

## Initial recommendation
**Do not fund the trading business on the current record.** Do not dismiss its quantum physics claim merely because a private cable exists. Instead, concede a narrow true possibility: within a deadline shorter than the best classical signal arrival, pre-distributed entanglement can outperform any local-classical strategy at this particular CHSH payoff game. We must investigate whether the measured violation is genuine, the resource can reach the desks in advance, and the customer faces a real payment function with the same structure.

## Most important uncertainty
The joint question: *What fraction of true end-user decisions (not just detector coincidences) take place where classical communication is too late, valid entanglement is already available, and a verified customer pays more for a CHSH-type joint outcome?*

The case does not provide the experimental denominator, long-haul delivery rate, exact deadline or market payoff mapping. Any one missing factor could invalidate the commercial thesis.

## Experiment plan
1. Derive best classical no-message, ideal quantum and one-bit classical outcomes with exact enumeration.
2. Map causal deadlines vs physical separation and optical attenuation.
3. Build a local-classical detection-postselection counterexample reproducing the apparent 82% score.
4. Reverse-engineer the claimed $50M *incremental* opportunity against the best alternative, not the startup's own 74% demo.
5. Change input odds to test dependence on the exact CHSH game structure.

## What would change my mind?
A documented event-ready loophole-resistant Bell result with independently random inputs; usable, measured distribution/storage of entangled states over the actual route; a real trading use case whose deadline excludes all feasible classical rivals; and a customer-backed incremental economic model after full delivery costs. A speculative certification pivot is separately worth exploring, not equivalent to accepting this investment.
