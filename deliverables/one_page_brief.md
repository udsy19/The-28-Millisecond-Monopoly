# One-page brief: entanglement-based coordination for trading desks

**Ask:** $3M at $25M pre-money ($28M post-money, a 10.7% stake).

**Recommendation: do not invest at this valuation.**

## Three numbers

1. **64.1%.** That is the win rate of the startup's 82% source on its own 40 km link, counting every round. The free classical rule wins 75%. A trading desk cannot discard rounds, so the product loses to doing nothing clever (E2).
2. **100%.** That is the win rate with one classical bit sent over the desks' existing private line. New York–London has 18.6 ms of light-travel time, and Hibernia Express delivers a bit in about 29.5 ms one way. With any time to communicate, entanglement is beaten (E1, E4).
3. **−$21.82 per round.** The startup's own payoff model, redone with every-round accounting on the 40 km link: at the target volume it loses about $78M a year instead of earning $50M, and $50M was already 1.0% of the whole ~$5B global latency-arbitrage pie (E6).

## Why

- **Photon loss is the lead finding.** Fibre loses photons. On 40 km only 40% reach each desk, and both arrive in only 16% of rounds. A desk without its photon still has to answer. The 82% headline and the 40 km link cannot both hold for a trading desk. The 82% was most likely counted only on rounds where both photons were detected (E2).
- **An 82% proves nothing by itself.** A classical setup that may drop rounds scores exactly 82% on the rounds it keeps (E3).
- **The usable zone is tiny.** Quantum only beats 75% within 5.7 km and under 19 µs. Every real trading route we checked is red or grey on our main figure (`figures/main_frontier.png`) (E4).
- **Signals must be balanced.** The edge vanishes unless each signal fires between 29% and 71% of the time. No trade has been named (E5).
- **IP risk.** US Patent 11676104B2 (Szegedy, Ding & Shi) describes the same idea: freedom to operate is unproven.

## What would reopen the deal

- Raw logs showing attempted, detected and discarded rounds, with a win rate above 75% over **all** attempted rounds.
- A named trade with a real payoff, balanced signals, and desks inside the green zone.
- A credible quantum-memory and heralding plan. Our model shows heralding keeps the win rate at or above 75% (E2b). The startup has not claimed memories, and heralding does nothing for New York–London.
- A freedom-to-operate opinion on the three filings, and a valuation that prices the company as an early hardware team, not a trading product.

## Pivot hypothesis (unproven)

The team's real asset is an entanglement source on deployed fibre. Certified randomness (provably unpredictable random numbers) and device-independent key distribution (keys secure even on untrusted hardware) value that without beating a classical bit, though both still need high detection efficiency. **We have not tested either market.**
