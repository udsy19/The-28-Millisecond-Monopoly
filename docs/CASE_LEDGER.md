## Explicit non-case modelling choices
- Distance roughly 5,570km is a geodesic scenario based on standard city coordinates, not actual cable length.
- Refractive index 1.468 and route multiplier 1.08 are transparent illustrative network parameters.
- Half of a historical <58.95ms RTT is only an equivalent one-way benchmark.
- Fibre attenuation 0.2 dB/km comes from published quantum-networking literature.
- A 1 pair/s minimum throughput and 1,000/s optimistic source are **exploratory thresholds**, not customer service requirements or facts.
- No quantum memory, repeater, satellite network, actual exchange, market impact, market fee structure, or live latency observation is supplied by the case.
- The biased-input experiment assumes 82% success on every input pair; this is not inferable from the aggregate 82% case report.

## Numerical derivations
Optimal local classical no-comm win = 0.75. Quantum expected game payoff = 0.82 × $100 + 0.18 × (−$100) − $0.08 = **$63.92**. Optimal classical expected game payoff = 0.75 × $100 + 0.25 × (−$100) − $0.001 = **$49.999**. Marginal toy-game edge = **$13.921 per round**. Claimed $50M / $13.921 ≈ **3,591,696 marginal monetizable rounds**. These are arithmetic implications of fiction, not revenues.

Full detection log, trial protocol, adversarial fairness assumptions, both detector efficiencies by setting, pair source vs received rate, all-round sample selection, fastest classical routing and latency, pre-shared state storage, measured long-haul fidelity/throughput, customer payoff mapping and annual qualifying transaction count.
