# Research charter: The 28-Millisecond Monopoly

## The commercial question
A fictional $25M pre-money quantum startup seeks $3M to sell entanglement-based coordination to London and New York trading desks. A 40km photonic demonstration reportedly achieves 82% success at a CHSH-like no-communication game; a startup-run classical demo reaches 74%. The claim is a $50M/year opportunity. The board asks: *does a scientific advantage create a defensible business?*

## Initial hypothesis (date: November 19, 2026)
**Provisional pass on the trading business; investigate a narrower scientific claim seriously.**

Prediction: the CHSH violation, if verified on all issued independent trials, is plausible; a physically meaningful no-communication deadline exists; direct fibre attenuation, missing Bell-test validation details and a missing trading payoff mapping make the proposed business uninvestable on the supplied record. We will not infer that any founder is dishonest.

## One creative insight
Faster communication and reliable quantum entanglement pull in opposite directions with distance: increased separation enlarges the interval before a classical message arrives, while unamplified telecom fibre exponentially destroys photon-pair throughput. We will show this with a causality × optics map. The 'gotcha' is not telephone access; it is the **missing commercial operating region**.

The second reveal is adversarial: a deterministic local response rule plus detector non-reporting can reproduce 82% coincidence-conditioned wins. The audience must ask what '1,000 trials' means.

## Hypotheses, potential falsifiers, and outputs
| ID | Testable hypothesis | Evidence that could falsify it | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-CHSH | Classical no-communication maximum is 75% on uniform settings. | Enumeration finds valid local strategy above 75%. | Exact 16-strategy table / tests. |
| H-CAUSAL | A tight decision regime exists where classical communication is impossible. | Faster known physical route invalidates a chosen deadline, or the payoff requires post-input communication. | Travel-time model / frontier. |
| H-OPTICS | Direct unrepeated ocean-spanning fibre cannot supply meaningful pair rates with a 1k/s order-of-magnitude source. | Valid transatlantic measured photon-pair delivery or radically different architecture. | Optical loss model / scenario record. |
| H-AUDIT | 82% could arise from selected coincidences under a local hidden-variable model. | A specified loophole-resistant all-round scoring protocol closes that selection avenue. | Analytic construction + seeded simulation. |
| H-MONEY | Claimed $50M in incremental profit is unsubstantiated by this game. | Customer verifies a CHSH-like profit function, per-round costs, 3.59M eligible annual decisions and route economics. | Economic counterfactual / sensitivity. |
| H-PIVOT | Verification/randomness might be worth a separate exploratory pilot. | Certification gates, market research or costs render even the alternative uneconomic. | Scoped optional product memo, never an automatic investment recommendation. |

## Required assignment phases
**Investigate:** initial decision, biggest unknown and what would change our mind.

**Experiment:** independently verifiable comparison of at least two relevant alternatives; code, table/figure and stated assumptions.

**Recommend:** short executive memo grounded in experiment, explicitly noting whether the original hypothesis survived.

## What we will not claim
A profitable real investment, transatlantic quantum trading latency advantage, secure keys from 82% alone, fraud from missing detector data, or actual causal influence transmitted by entanglement.

## Success criteria
All experiments regenerate from committed code and inputs; CI passes; the map distinguishes impossibility from route-dependent uncertainty; the audit explicitly labels sample denominators; results and memo agree; the decision has clear conditions for reversal; the exhibit remains compelling without hiding the caveats.
