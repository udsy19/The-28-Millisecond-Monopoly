# Creative direction: the investment war room

## Title
**The 28-Millisecond Monopoly**
Subtitle: **How a quantum startup can win the physics and lose the market.**

The title is an intentionally memorable approximation to a fibre-scale one-way NY–London communication time; it is **not** a universal measured or causality-exact 28ms threshold.

## Narrative approach
Make a detective story out of evidence. Every act first gives the founders a good-faith hearing, then produces the strongest alternative explanation or missing-data question. We do **not** put a fake founder in the role of villain.

## Five-act experience
**Act I — The claim.** Show $3M, $25M, 82%, 40km, 1,000/s and $50M. Present the challenge as a due diligence hearing.

**Act II — Steelman inside the light cone.** Show the exact CHSH classical optimum and the spacelike deadline argument. Place a timeline over a night skyline; indicate 18.6ms physical floor, ~27ms ideal fibre, historical under-58.95ms RTT. Emphasize correlations without signalling.

**Act III — The quantum loss wall.** The user drags distance and decision deadline. The interface exposes the conflict: longer distance makes messages slow but direct photon transmission very unlikely. Colour coding is physics-only, not profitability.

**Act IV — The forensic reveal.** With a single slider for the fraction of locally filtered rounds, a classical detector model reports the startup's 82%. Freeze-frame: **Which denominator did the founders count?** Provide the complete issued-trial count.

**Act V — The money.** Start with the founder's claimed $63.92 expected fictional per-round net payoff; replace their 74% demo with the optimal 75% classical baseline, producing the marginal $13.921 and a 3.59M annual volume threshold. Distort the referee's input odds; watch the edge disappear under an explicitly fixed quantum-strategy hypothesis. The investment memo closes with falsifiable reopening requirements.

## Visual language
Dark editorial research interface, cool indigo for physics possibility, warm amber for missing assumptions, coral for direct-fibre infeasibility, teal for classical solution. Typography must emphasize contrast between *claims* and *findings*. Interaction should be local, responsive, privacy-safe, keyboard accessible and functional without third-party APIs.

## Distinctive artifacts
- Interactive causal × loss 'frontier' heatmap.
- Classical post-selection 'counterfeit' demonstration.
- Clean Excel-compatible CSV tables from the numerical engine.
- SVG figures suitable for a one-page investment memo.
- A 'strongest founder rebuttal' appendix to prevent confirmation bias.
- A 'three mandatory gates' investment conclusion, not a sensational fraud accusation.

## Three-minute oral defense
0:00 — 'We gave the quantum startup the strongest possible argument.'
0:35 — Show why light speed creates a legitimate correlation-only time window.
1:05 — Show how pair delivery collapses with distance.
1:35 — Demonstrate the 82% classical detection-loophole model.
2:05 — Replace the 74% marketing baseline with the 75% theoretical competitor.
2:35 — Conclude: scientifically plausible, commercially unverified, with clear evidence required to reverse the decision.

**Principle:** Visual creativity must make an independent audit easier, not replace it.
