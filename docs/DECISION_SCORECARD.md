# Independent investment decision scorecard

## Required gates
| Category | Current case evidence | Underwrite threshold | Default state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific advantage | 82% claimed / 1,000 'trials', undefined denominator | Full issued-event log, random independent inputs, no-click treatment, loophole-aware confidence bound, reproducible protocol | UNVERIFIED |
| Unique operational edge | Unspecified decision deadline; private classical line exists | Real use-case decision must terminate before best classical route can deliver the necessary bit | UNVERIFIED |
| Quantum network | 40km link, 1,000 pairs/s ambiguously specified | Independently measured end-to-end usable pairs, fidelity, distribution costs, storage/buffer, availability, synchronized use | UNVERIFIED |
| Revenue | Artificial ±$100 CHSH referee, claimed $50M | Customer-approved unit economics, actual annual eligible rounds, market impact/friction, implementation cost, risk adjustment | UNVERIFIED |
| Defensibility | Founders from national lab, 3 patents filed | FTO/patent claim review, actual moat, procurement evidence, customer alternatives | UNVERIFIED |

## Decision rule
NO-GO for a $3M investment in the trading pitch unless the first four gates are independently supported. A narrow feasibility option can be contemplated only with milestones and staged payments. An impressive paper alone is insufficient.

## Conditions that could change the current no-go
1. A verified loophole-resistant violation and independent audit showing all issued rounds and random input provenance.
2. Measured usable entanglement delivery with pre-sharing/storage before actual inputs and reliability at the decision rate.
3. A concrete CHSH-like trade opportunity no one-bit classical or pre-agreed randomness strategy can serve before the actual deadline.
4. Positive net *incremental* customer value after total deployment, trading friction, failures and opportunity frequency.
5. A realistic price and procurement route where the customer, not a synthetic referee, pays.

## Distinguish verdicts
- Scientific: **POSSIBLE BUT NOT VERIFIED** on the supplied record.
- Causality: **REAL PHYSICAL REGIME**, but actual route/deadline unresolved.
- Direct fibre at 5,600 km: **NOT CREDIBLE UNDER THE MODEL** (not all quantum networking).
- Economic product: **UNDEMONSTRATED**, not logically impossible.
- Financial recommendation: **PASS ON CURRENT TERMS; REOPEN ON EVIDENCE**.
