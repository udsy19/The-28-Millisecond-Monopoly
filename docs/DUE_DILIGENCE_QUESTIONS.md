# Investment committee interrogation list

## A. Bell experiment and anti-postselection
1. Exactly how is an 'issued trial' defined? Does 1,000 count source pulses, heralded pairs, coincident detections, or scored settings?
2. Can we inspect machine-readable x, y, a, b, local timestamps and both detectors' no-click events for every issued trial?
3. Are x and y independent, truly generated after any shared resource is fixed, and free from setting predictability?
4. What is detection efficiency conditional on each local setting x and y?
5. Did any coincidence-window, heralding criterion or postselection depend on local outcome or setting?
6. How were no-click rounds assigned outputs or included in the test statistic?
7. Which statistical test was prespecified? What is the uncertainty and p-value under allowed memory/drift/adaptive strategies?
8. Are measurement-event space-time separations sufficient to address locality?
9. Was the 74% classical comparison best-possible or simply the startup's own demo?
10. Was the reported 82% independently repeated, and what is the raw protocol/source code?

## B. Photon network
11. Does 1,000 entangled pairs/s mean generated, detected at one end, detected jointly, or ready to use after all heralding?
12. What are dark-count, misalignment, efficiency, synchronization and fidelity numbers, by condition and distance?
13. Where are the entangled resources physically produced and how do both desks receive them?
14. Can the resource be reliably distributed and stored **before** input bits arrive?
15. Is there a quantum memory? What are storage lifetime, decoherence, buffer size, and refill time?
16. What route reaches both desks? Direct fibre, trusted relay, repeater, or satellite? What has actually been demonstrated?
17. Why are conventional telecom amplifiers incompatible with naive single-photon transmission?
18. What are usable **joint detection/decision** events per second over the actual operational route?
19. What happens to real trade decisions during photon outages?
20. What does a validated month of operation cost per usable decision?

## C. Real trading payoff
21. Who supplies the two supposedly independent random input bits? How are they related to economically meaningful orders?
22. Why is the loss function exactly a⊕b=x·y and why are inputs uniform?
23. What is the best achievable policy with **pre-shared classical randomness**?
24. How quickly can one bit traverse the best available classical radio, microwave, optical, or fibre route under contractual conditions?
25. When is the *actual* decision deadline? Is 18–30ms relevant, and to which market event?
26. How are failed correlation outcomes converted into actual slippage or trade P&L?
27. Who pays the $100 on success, and bears the $100 loss; where do these cashflows originate?
28. How many qualifying, independently paid decisions exist annually, after market overlap and risk limits?
29. Are costs inclusive of hardware capital, support, compliance, colocation, transmissions, missed trades and adverse selection?
30. What prevents a competitor from adopting the same protocol if the patents are applications rather than issued and enforceable claims?

## D. Founder rebuttals worth taking seriously
31. Is a true delay advantage possible with *prepositioned* entanglement, even when per-second ocean distribution is tiny?
32. Could a geographically closer market opportunity preserve the light-cone regime while allowing more realistic pair delivery?
33. Could a pre-certified entropy/randomness source be independently useful, if valid assumptions hold?
34. What minimal scientific milestone can be financed without committing the full $3M?
35. What empirical finding would the founders themselves accept as disproving their business thesis?
