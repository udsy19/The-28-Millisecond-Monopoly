# Experiment plan: designed to change the decision

## Primary design question
Does any meaningful New York–London trading decision have a deadline and payoff for which pre-distributed nonlocal correlations outperform every *deployable* classical alternative, after delivery failure, delay and operating cost?

## Phase 1 — initial thesis
Do not invest in the stated trading product on the fictional record; it is intellectually possible for a quantum game to have a real edge when classical post-input messaging cannot arrive. The question is whether the physics, network and market intersection is populated by real demand. Falsifier: valid loophole-free all-trial data, operational pair availability in the geographic regime, and an actual cash-generating CHSH-like payoff verified by a customer.

## Experiments (phase 2)
### E1. Exact classical game benchmark (deterministic)
Enumerate every deterministic strategy (a0,a1,b0,b1) in {0,1}^4. Score four input pairs. Confirm max 3/4. Show classical with the one-bit message x can output a=0, b=x*y and always win. Quantitative comparison: 74% reported classical, 75% optimal classical, 82% reported quantum, 85.36% quantum ideal. Validation: 16 strategies and per-input one-bit truth table.

### E2. Statistical inference under conditional observability (analytical)
If the 1,000 are issued, complete fair independent input rounds, compare 820+ wins to Binomial(1,000,0.75) upper tail. Report ~7.5e-8 **only with this conditional assumption**. If 1,000 is postselected coincidence sample, leave the hypothesis unresolved. Validation: exact integer sum and unit test.

### E3. The causal frontier (numerical sensitivity)
Geodesic ~5,570km; compute the vacuum lower-bound one-way time and straight fibre assumed time. Compare decision deadlines 0.05–40ms and paths 40–5,600km. Label three communication regimes: physically excluded, fibre-model route dependent, and a classical bit arrives. Real fastest routes are not assumed known.

### E4. The optical survival surface (numerical sensitivity)
Under 0.2dB/km fibre loss, optical transmission is 10^(-alpha * distance / 10). Model both interpretations of 1,000/s at 40km: hypothetical 1,000 source photons/s vs hypothetical 1,000 *received* pairs/s. Display log-scale rate; no repeaters/satellites/memories. Explicitly label a 1 pair/s threshold as illustrative, not a buyer requirement.

### E5. Classical '82%' counterfeit (analytic + Monte Carlo)
Use a=b=0 (classical true win 75%). On a shared-random q fraction of rounds, Alice's local detector reports no-click for x=1. Bob always reports. The retained fraction is 1 - q/2; conditional wins (0.75 - 0.25q)/(1 - q/2). For q=0.4375, this is 82% and 78.125% retention. Simulate 100,000 issued trials with fixed seed. This is **not** fraud evidence and is not a claim about the startup's optical experiment.

### E6. Incremental profit audit (deterministic arithmetic)
Compare expected fictional game payoff for observed quantum 82%, startup demo 74%, optimal classical 75%, and one-bit perfect classical 100%. Quantify $13.921 marginal edge over optimal no-comm classical and ceil($50M/$13.921) annual monetizable rounds. Costs exclude deployment and market frictions: any apparent profit is only a toy scenario.

### E7. Biased-referee payoff surgery (numerical stress test)
Independently vary p=P(x=1)=P(y=1) from .1 to .5. Recalculate exact optimal classical success 1-min(Pxy). **Assume** a fixed per-setting 82% quantum strategy and show where the incremental toy payoff reverses. Do not infer this from the aggregate 82% claim or imply it's a universal quantum optimum.

### E8. Research extension: operational market model (to implement)
Construct a real decision-time/payoff ontology: when are bits observed, what action is chosen, when does settlement occur, is communication/consistency required, is the payoff symmetrical, and which risk-adjusted return metric is realizable? Compare classical pre-agreed randomness, classical one-bit messaging, any alternate faster route, and pre-distributed entanglement. Only model hypothetical archetypes until actual market data are found.

### E9. Research extension: rebuttal and pivot (to implement)
Give the founding team its strongest rebuttal: event-ready Bell test, quantum-memory buffer, satellite/repeater link, rare deadline-sensitive opportunities. For each, identify one measurement and a contract milestone that would materially change the recommendation. Explore a *separate* certification product; do not claim 82% certifies randomness or secure keys.

## Reproducibility & outputs
Build with make check. Independently inspect artifacts/metrics.json, artifacts/tables/*.csv, artifacts/figures/*.svg, artifacts/EXECUTIVE_MEMO.md. Source and scenario register: docs/EVIDENCE_POLICY.md. Every model is falsifiable by changing assumptions; the experiment is not designed to 'prove the startup wrong' in advance.

## Biggest uncertainty
Not the value of light speed. It is whether the 82% result was a fair all-round Bell test *and* whether any customer values the same task under deadlines that rule out cheaper classical solutions. A report containing the missing detector and market details would have more decision value than another decimal place of fibre loss.
