The Photon That Didn't Show Up

Two trading desks play the CHSH game: each sees one secret bit and must answer without talking to the other. The best classical plan wins 75% of rounds. Shared entangled photons can win up to 85.4%, but only if both photons actually arrive. Move the sliders to see where that edge survives fibre loss and the speed of light.

Photon delivery

Non-heralded: a desk whose photon is lost doesn't know whether the other desk got one, so many rounds become coin flips. Heralded: a signal says in advance whether a good pair is stored, so lost pairs fall back to the 75% classical rule at no cost.

Share of rounds that start with a confirmed, stored pair. (both photons survive this link).

How long the desks have to answer. If it is longer than light takes to cross between desks, one ordinary message settles the game.

Unbalanced signals

The textbook game flips each desk's bit 50/50. Real signals rarely are. With perfect photons, how much room is left for quantum?
