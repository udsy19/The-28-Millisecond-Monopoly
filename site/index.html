01 / THE STEELMAN

Give them their best argument.

Two trading desks receive independent private inputs. Each must answer before communicating. They win the CHSH game when their outputs agree, except on the 11 input.

Without communication, the best classical strategy wins 75%. Ideal entangled qubits can reach 85.36%. The fictional team reports 82%.

That is interesting. It does not allow faster-than-light signalling. The exploitable resource, if any, is pre-distributed correlation under a strict deadline.

03 / THE ADVERSARIAL AUDIT

Make 82% without entanglement.

The counterexample uses an entirely local strategy. Both desks normally output 0, winning three of four equally likely setting pairs. On some preselected rounds, Alice's detector stays silent when her local input equals 1.

If an analyst counts only detected pairs, the apparent success rate rises—even though the strategy remains classical. This is an illustration of a detection/post-selection loophole, not a claim that the fictional founders cheated.
